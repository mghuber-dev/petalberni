Protect Earth

Human dignity, ecological survival, and the responsibility to care for our only living world.

“The strongest case for Earth is not anti-science. It is the claim that intelligence should first protect the living world that made intelligence possible.”

Earth argument 1

The only habitable world we know

Earth combines atmosphere, oceans, climate stability, soils, and ecosystems in ways no known planet matches. Complex life depends on conditions that took billions of years to develop.

The hard question is not whether this matters, but how to act quickly, fairly, and globally without using urgency as an excuse for poor decisions or narrow political control.

Earth argument 2

Climate change and adaptation

Reducing emissions must happen alongside adaptation: cooler cities, protected coastlines, resilient food and water systems, and support for communities facing the greatest risk.

The hard question is not whether this matters, but how to act quickly, fairly, and globally without using urgency as an excuse for poor decisions or narrow political control.

Earth argument 3

Poverty and preventable suffering

Hunger, housing insecurity, unsafe water, sanitation gaps, education barriers, and preventable disease make a powerful moral claim on resources now.

Biodiversity and ecosystem collapse

Forests, wetlands, pollinators, oceans, and species diversity are not decorative. Human food, health, and stability rely on functioning ecosystems.

The limits of escaping
