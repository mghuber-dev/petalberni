Sproat Lake Provincial Park

Sproat Lake

A popular lakefront provincial park west of Port Alberni with forested trails, day-use areas, and beach access. It is a beautiful stop for leashed walks and lake views, but dogs should not be assumed to be allowed on the main beach or swimming areas.

AreaSproat Lake, west of Port Alberni

Pet guidanceBC Parks generally requires pets to be leashed and under control, and pets are not allowed in beach areas or park buildings unless posted otherwise.

Best forLeashed forest walks, lake views, picnic stops, short trails.

Before you go

Pet rules can change with seasons, wildlife activity, maintenance, and posted park notices. Bring a leash, bags, water, and choose a different stop if signs restrict pets in beach, playground, swimming, or sensitive habitat areas.
